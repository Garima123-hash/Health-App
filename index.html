Prototype note: CareSphere is a local prototype. Accounts, hospital workspaces, appointments, doctor listings, patient-owned report uploads, prescription and invoice records, and dashboard summaries use the MongoDB-backed API. Optional Women’s Health logging and its local source-retrieval guide are not AI, cannot diagnose PCOS/PCOD or interpret medical history, and do not provide treatment advice. The report overview only extracts selectable PDF text locally; it cannot read scans or images. Password reset, real payment processing, and medical integrations are not provided. Do not use real patient information or rely on this app for medical decisions.
